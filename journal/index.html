From Thin Air: journal

A 3D modeling dataset built from thin air.

This journal is the official record of the project. Every step gets a page. A page says what the step was for, what was done, what we saw, and what comes next.

How the journal works

  • One page per step. Pages are numbered in the order the work happened.
  • The page comes before the next step. A step is not finished until its page exists.
  • Every page has the same sections. Goal, what was done, what we saw, what it means, open questions, next step.
  • Every page has a status. "Proposed" means the owner has not agreed yet. "Agreed" means the owner has read it and said yes.
  • Every generation run is timed. The times go on the page, with the machine they ran on. The owner set this rule on 2026-11-25.
  • Nothing is built on a proposed page. The next step starts only after the owner agrees.
